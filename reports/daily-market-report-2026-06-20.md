## 2. MARKET TREND PREDICTIONS

### CORN — Bearish Near-Term
- December corn broke below $4.40 support (the August 2025 lows), indicating further downside risk.
- USDA crop ratings show 67% good-to-excellent condition; 97% planted, 86% emerged.
- Abundant rain across the Corn Belt and below-normal temps point to strong early crop development.
- Prices are near or below breakeven cost of production, which may limit further downside.
- **Trend: DOWN** — oversold conditions may attract some short-covering, but fundamentals remain bearish.

### SOYBEANS — Bearish to Neutral
- Planting at 92% (ahead of 5-year average of 88%), emergence at 79% (vs. 71% avg).
- Quality ratings slipped slightly to 65% good-to-excellent.
- Price upside likely capped by expectations USDA's June 30 Acreage report will show sharp upward revision in soybean plantings.
- Second-largest soybean crop in US history projected at 4.435 billion bushels.
- **Trend: SIDEWAYS TO DOWN** — large supply outlook weighing on prices.

### WHEAT — Bullish
- Winter wheat quality ratings severely depressed at only 25% good-to-excellent.
- SRW wheat showing strength with recent gains.
- Technical indicators flashing "Strong Buy" signal.
- Poor crop conditions domestically could tighten supply.
- **Trend: UP** — poor crop quality is the strongest bullish catalyst in the grain complex.

### MILK — Bearish
- All-milk price projected at $19.70/cwt for 2026 (down from $21.17 in 2025).
- Rising milk production and component output pressuring prices.
- Class III expected to average $16.75/cwt in H2 2026.
- High volatility expected from temporary supply/demand imbalances.
- **Trend: DOWN** — production growth outpacing demand.

### BEEF/CATTLE — Bullish
- Record-high prices driven by historically low cattle inventories.
- US cattle imports from Mexico suspended (screwworm outbreak).
- 2026 beef production forecast lowered to 25.438 billion lbs.
- Wildfires and labor stoppages creating additional supply pressure.
- **Trend: UP** — tight supply fundamentals remain firmly in place.

### LEAN HOGS — Neutral to Bearish
- Prices fell 3.13% over the past month, down 14.51% YoY.
- Recent session showed a bounce (+1.52%), but overall trend is lower.
- 2027 prices projected to decline 2.7% from 2026 levels.
- **Trend: SIDEWAYS TO DOWN** — seasonal strength fading.

### LAMB/SHEEP — Bullish
- Prices up 53% YoY, averaging $262.40/cwt in April 2026.
- Seasonal peak typically occurs in summer months — further upside possible.
- Limited futures trading makes hedging challenging.
- **Trend: UP** — strong demand and seasonal factors supportive.
